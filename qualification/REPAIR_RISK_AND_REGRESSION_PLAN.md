# Repair risk and regression plan

The proposed VERIFY admission gate could suppress a genuinely useful test or
configuration neighbor when relation extraction is incomplete. It must not
touch PRIMARY, hard-denied paths, exact passthrough, support budgets, observer
semantics, or route-mode thresholds.

Before any implementation, test: duplicate basenames, source/test pairs,
cross-package imports, config/build relations, generated-source exceptions,
explicit paths, weak-evidence abstention, and ordinary localized edits. Compare
success, required-path recall, unused-path precision, searches, requests,
complete-task tokens, p95 cost, and unsafe outcomes. Reject if recall falls,
ordinary tasks regress, or savings are obtained only by excessive abstention.
