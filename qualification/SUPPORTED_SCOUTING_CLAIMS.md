# Supported scouting claims

- On this 30-task synthetic development corpus, B0 and STANDARD each completed 27 tasks.
- B0 reduced agent tokens by 7.8%, requests by 15.2%, unique reads by 25.8%, and searches by 31.0% in the one-repetition primary panel.
- B0 increased repeated reads and had a worse p95 token tail.
- One B0 quality-and-efficiency win reproduced 3/3 versus 0/3 for STANDARD.
- One B0 quality regression reproduced 0/3 versus 3/3 for STANDARD.
- The regression has a high-confidence causal chain through lower-authority duplicate exposure, but only one unique task reproduced harm.
- Three tasks establish a high-confidence unrelated-verification-path cost cluster that clears the future repair rule.
- B0 abstention was quality-neutral in aggregate on the observed abstain cells, but abstention correctness was not independently adjudicated.
- All finally counted B0 cells had native COMPLETE receipts and no recommendation-safety failure.
- Recorded wall time excludes B0 compiler overhead and is not complete-task wall time.
- The evidence supports one future repair target; it does not promote a new baseline.
