# B0 scouting summary

Status: `scouting_complete_with_measurement_debt`

This development study compared frozen B0 guidance with an untreated STANDARD Qwen agent. It is diagnostic evidence, not release or marketing qualification.

## Study integrity

- 30 tasks across 6 independently structured synthetic repositories
- Python, TypeScript/JavaScript, Swift, and documentation/configuration surfaces
- 60-run primary panel: 30 B0 and 30 STANDARD
- 72 targeted repeat runs, selected only for discordance, material cost, or causal stability
- 132 counted analytical runs; 62 superseded-validator or observer-invalid runs preserved and excluded
- 3 calibration smoke runs
- all 66 finally counted B0 runs produced genuinely native COMPLETE receipts
- zero policy-denied recommendations, zero runtime failures, and zero fixture reuse
- development designation; every task is excluded from future held-out release proof

## Primary-panel outcome

Both arms completed 27 of 30 tasks. B0 used 237,822 agent tokens versus 257,942 for STANDARD, a reduction of 20,120 (7.8%). B0 also used 32 fewer requests, 31 fewer unique reads, and 9 fewer searches. B0 repeated reads more often and had a worse token tail: p95 agent tokens were 13,654 for B0 versus 12,623 for STANDARD.

The aggregate tie hides two reproducible quality effects:

- one B0 quality-and-efficiency win reproduced 3/3 versus 0/3 for STANDARD;
- one B0 regression reproduced 0/3 versus 3/3 for STANDARD.

## Decision

The next repair priority is stricter verification-path qualification. Across three tasks in two layout families, B0 supplied unrelated verification paths that the agent read. All three showed preserved-quality cost regressions; the largest added a median 11,188 tokens, 6 requests, and 4 reads.

The largest quality defect remains lower-authority duplicate exposure: one task reproduced B0 0/3 versus STANDARD 3/3. Because harm was confirmed on only one unique task, it does not independently clear the multi-task repair rule.

Wall-time results cover agent execution only. B0 compilation occurred before timing and remains explicit measurement debt.
